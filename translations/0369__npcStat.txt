!{
For setting the $npcID, $npcdesc, dick, $dick_girth, etc. with a single call for a npc
Used for translating generated variables (like npc_haircol) to useable strings.
Use with a gosub and match the ARGS[0] with the correct char
A generated NPC would be gs 'npcStat', $npclastcalled
Other NPCs may be called either by number if they are a static NPC, or by letter and number otherwise.
eg. "gs 'npcStat', 'A56'" would call for Roma. Likewise, "gs 'npcStat', 'A56'" also calls for Roma.
A second argument calls for multiple partners. They are output as:
e.g. gs 'npcStat', 'A54', 'a' would set $npcID1 to 'A54'
	'a' for stats1
	'b' for stats2
	'c' for stats3
	'd' for stats4
	'e' for stats5
	'f' for stats6
	'g' for stats7
	'h' for stats8
	'i' for stats9
	'j'-'z' are reserved for internal functions and can, and will, be overwritten.
		DO NOT USE 'j'-'z' for events!!!
If ARGS[1] is not set it defaults to zero, so you can use the variables it sets with or without [0] e.g. dick or dick[0] are equivalent
$ARGS[2] is the condom type. Current types are:
	- 'none':			no condom
	- 'normal':			a normal condom
	- 'sabotaged'		a sabotaged condom
	- 'sabotaged_other'	a condom sabotaged by another npc (aka not Sveta)
	- 'broken'			a broken condom
	- 'extra_thin'		a thin condom (more likely to break)
	- 'ribbed'			a ribbed condom

eg. to equip a male NPC with an extra thin condom you would call:
	gs 'npcStat', $npcID, 0, 'extra_thin'
to equip two condoms of different types on two different male NPCs, you could call:
	gs 'npcStat', $npcID, 0, 'normal'
	gs 'npcStat', $npcID, 1, 'ribbed'
	to set dick[0] with a normal condom and dick[1] with a ribbed condom
or you could call 
	gs 'npcStat', $npcID, 0, 'normal'
	gs 'npcStat', $npcID, 'a', 'extra_thin'
	gs 'npcStat', $npcID, 'b', 'ribbed'
	to set dick1 with a normal condom and dick2 with a thin condom and dick3 with a ribbed condom
!}

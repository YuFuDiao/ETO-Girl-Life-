!! Swan Lake - Composed by Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky with choreography originally set by Julius Reisinger, but most famously staged later by Marius Petipa and Lev Ivanov.

!! The Nutcracker - Also composed by Tchaikovsky, this ballet is often performed during the holiday season. Its choreography has been reimagined many times but remains
!! closely associated with productions set to Tchaikovsky''s enchanting score.

!! Giselle - With music by Adolphe Adam and original choreography by Jean Coralli and Jules Perrot, this romantic ballet tells a poignant tale of love and redemption in
!! the supernatural realm.

!! The Sleeping Beauty - Another masterpiece by Tchaikovsky with choreography by Marius Petipa. This opulent work is known for its intricate sets, costumes, and depiction

!! Don Quixote – Composed by Ludwig Minkus with later contributions from Alexander Glazunov and Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov, this ballet captures Cervantes'' story in a lively
!! and colorful production featuring elaborate sets and costumes.

!! Romeo and Juliet - Music by Sergei Prokofiev with choreography by Sergei Radlov, Kenneth MacMillan, or John Cranko, depending on the version. This adaptation of
!! Shakespeare’s tragic love story is both dramatic and emotionally stirring.

!! The Firebird - A one-act ballet composed by Igor Stravinsky, originally choreographed by Michel Fokine for Serge Diaghilev''s Ballets Russes. It blends Russian folklore
!! with striking music and vibrant stagecraft.

!! Carmen Suite – Based on the opera "Carmen" by Georges Bizet, it was adapted into a ballet by Roland Petit in 1949. The piece is renowned for its passionate storytelling
